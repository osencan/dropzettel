# Start Here

This vault is filed by [Dropzettel](https://github.com/osencan/dropzettel). You capture; it files, tags, links and logs.

## Your part

1. **Capture.** Put anything into the inbox folder: a link, a thought, a screenshot, a PDF. Add one sentence of why if you can.
2. **Check.** Open `0 - Dashboard/To Check` once or twice a week. Deal with an item, then set its `status` to `done`.
3. **Think.** The weekly review proposes one to three notes worth writing in your own words. Write them in `4 - Zettelkasten`. Nothing automatic ever writes there.

## The folders

| Folder | What is in it | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| `0 - Dashboard` | Capture Log, To Check, Tag Registry, Weekly Reviews | Dropzettel |
| `1 - Raw Notes` | Your thoughts, ideas, questions, as captured | Dropzettel, from your words |
| `2 - Sources` | One note per link, video, paper, tool, file | Dropzettel |
| `3 - Indexes` | One hub note per topic, listing its captures | Dropzettel appends; you can reorganise |
| `4 - Zettelkasten` | Permanent notes: one idea each, in your words | **Only you** |
| `5 - Templates` | Templates for the notes you write | You |
| `6 - Attachments` | Images, PDFs and files that came through the inbox | Dropzettel |

## Reading the log

`0 - Dashboard/Capture Log YYYY-MM` has one entry per capture: what came in, where it was filed, the tags (and which are new), the topic, related notes, and the reason. If something was filed wrongly, fix the note; the tag registry rebuilds itself.
